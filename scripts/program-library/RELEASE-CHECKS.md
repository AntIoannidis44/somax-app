# Content checks before a PUBLIC release

Fine for TestFlight testers; resolve before the App Store / any paid tier.

## Held back (not in the app)
- `meals/held/ais-sports-nutrition-meals.json` - 103 AIS recipes, CC BY-NC-ND. Needs written
  permission from the Australian Sports Commission (copyright request form on ausport.gov.au).
- `meals/held/efh-meals.json` - 10 Eat For Health (NHMRC) recipes; site says CC BY 4.0 but the
  recipe pages don't carry the mark. Confirm with NHMRC or leave held.
- Not taken at all (would need permission): Football Australia Perform+, NSW Health Healthy
  Ageing manual, Heart Foundation, Better Health Channel, Diabetes Australia, Canada's Food Guide.

## In the app - confirm terms
- NHS (programs + recipes): Open Government Licence v3.0 - the app shows the required line on
  NHS items; confirm none of the used pages are excluded from the OGL.
- NHLBI recipes: public domain; required credit line is shown. No logos, no implied endorsement.
- VA Healthy Teaching Kitchen recipes: US federal work (17 U.S.C. 105); no endorsement implied.
- StrengthLog (21 sessions), running festival / club PDFs, Musashi AFL (9): copyright notices but
  no reuse terms either way. We store facts only (exercises, sets, reps, times), all prose is ours,
  credited + linked. Consider a courtesy email to each.
- Brand names (StrongLifts, 5/3/1, HYROX, StrongFirst, Brooks, Kieser...) are credits only - the
  app must not imply endorsement.
- HYROX: Somax sessions use Open-division weights; credited sessions keep the coach's weights as
  published. Check against the current HYROX rulebook before calling anything "official".
- Safety copy (HIIT gating, older adults, postnatal, open water, concussion) - clinician review.
